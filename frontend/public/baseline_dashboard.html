Final Report implication: Both methods remain as the two viable signal-carrying method families — neither dominates the other in overall MAE (within noise). LLM wins on overall MAE (0.864 vs 0.877); SigLIP2 wins on structure Pearson r (+0.407 vs +0.266). The new finding from scaling: LLM-as-judge shows calibration instability under distribution shift on structure, while ranking signal is preserved — a caveat for production deployment.
